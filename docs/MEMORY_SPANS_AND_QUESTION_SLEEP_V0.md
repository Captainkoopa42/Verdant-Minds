# Memory spans in experiences, and questions steering sleep with revision underneath (v0)

Status: PREREGISTERED 2026-11-20 before the runs. Results appended below.

## Step 1: memory spans in experiences
Sleep's horizon ("stop rehearsing what has not really recurred for 400 cycles") is the only part
of the running stack timed in kernel cycles. Kernel cycles also advance for bookkeeping (each
structural challenge, refold and Council sitting), so revision quietly shortened memory. The
horizon is now optionally counted in real experiences (`--horizon-experiences 76`: the same 400
cycles at ~5.3 cycles per experience; the length is inherited, only the clock changes).
H1: on changing life 9001, the spaced_long gap between `revise` and `watch` (promoted -2, kept -6
with the cycle clock) shrinks to within 1 promoted and 2 kept. If not, the cycle coupling is not
the cause and that is recorded.

## Step 2: questions steer sleep, revision underneath (fork)
Stack: Council `ready` + heat `revise` + stored questions direct sleep (`--inquiry-authority
sleep`: only stalled questions whose single failing gate is internal cohesion), horizon in
experiences, clock sleep (the gear is not better than the clock yet, so it is left out to keep
one change at a time). Compared against `revise` alone, same horizon, on four lives (changing
9001 and 4242, old 9001 and 4242).
Gates: kept and promoted on the passing groups not lower on any life; active wrong 0; changed
lives still 5/5 revised and no control touched; no promotion from a failing group.
Prediction: spaced_long promoted rises on at least 3 of the 4 lives.

## Results (appended)

### Step 1: H1 met in full
| changing 9001 | spaced_long promoted / kept | revised |
|---|---|---|
| watch, cycle horizon | 6 / 38 | - |
| revise, cycle horizon | 4 / 32 | 5/5 |
| watch, experience horizon | 6 / 38 | - |
| revise, experience horizon | **6 / 38** | 5/5 |

With the horizon counted in experiences, revision costs nothing: revise and watch give identical
scores on every passing group. The whole revision cost was the cycle coupling. (Watch itself
gives the same scores under either clock; its fingerprint differs because Council sittings also
advance cycles.)

### Step 2: questions steer sleep, revision underneath (fork)
Passing groups (massed, spaced_short, spaced_long, returning); "wrong" is the historic count
(retired folds included); active wrong structures at the end were 0 in every full-stack run.

| life | revise only (kept / promoted / spaced_long) | full stack (kept / promoted / spaced_long) | sleep slots asked by questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| changing 9001 | 136 / 37 / 6 | **145 / 43 / 12** | 153 |
| changing 4242 | 139 / 40 / 9 | **148 / 43 / 12** | 95 |
| old 9001 | 145 / 42 / 11 | **148 / 44 / 13** | 38 |
| old 4242 | 148 / 46 / 15 | 148 / 46 / 15 (already full) | 34 |

All gates met: no drop on any life; 5/5 revisions and no control touched on both changing lives;
no promotion from a failing group anywhere; active wrong 0. Prediction met: spaced_long rises on
3 of 4 lives (the fourth was already 15/15). Same number of sleeps as before (68 / 50): the
questions change what sleep practises, not how often.

## What this means
The knife-edge long-gap families were bonds that existed but faded between sightings. A stored
question that names exactly that gap, allowed to choose what sleep practises, keeps them strong
enough to pass when the world brings them back, while heat revision underneath catches any
pattern that should not have been kept. Together, on four lives: more kept, more promoted, no
wrong patterns in use, and nothing done on lives where nothing called for it.
