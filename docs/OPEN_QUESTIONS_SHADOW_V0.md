# Open questions in shadow v0 (wiring plan step 6), and a forked test of authority

Status: shadow RESULTS; fork PREREGISTERED below before it ran. `sidecar_inquiry`.

## What runs
After every real experience two watchers look at the organism and write nothing into it:
1. the obligation substrate's five detectors, through `inspect` only (every 10 experiences);
2. a developmental bridge: every structure candidate that recurs but is not eligible becomes an
   open question naming the structure stage's own failing gates; untouched for 20 experiences
   it is stalled into cold storage with a sha256 certificate (snapshot + gates + reopen
   condition "observed again"); when the candidate is observed again it wakes and is rechecked;
   it resolves when the structure stage marks it eligible.

## Fork test, PREREGISTERED: may the stored questions direct sleep?
A forked organism (separate run; the real one is untouched) where stalled questions whose only
failing gate is internal cohesion choose what sleep rehearses first (`--inquiry-authority
sleep`). All rehearsal safety rules stay (staged, all-or-nothing, no creation, real exposures
>= 1, no candidate change); only the generic "fading below 0.26, within 400 cycles" filter is
lifted for the questioned candidate's own internal bonds.

Gates: wrong promotions 0; no family from single_context / under_exposed / once promoted;
kept >= 145/148, promoted >= 43/46.
Prediction: at least 1 of the 3 missed spaced-long families is promoted (its question
resolves when the world brings it back with cohesion now >= 0.38). Failure modes named in
advance: the families do not recur after they stall (then sleep cannot help, the reward never
arrives), or directed sleep starves the ordinary rehearsal and costs kept cues elsewhere.

## What this does and does not establish
* Established: the stored questions are accurate (they resolve exactly when, and only for, the
  families that pass), they correctly separate what the world must supply from what the
  organism can supply itself, and on two lives giving them one narrow power (choosing what
  sleep rehearses) plus a self-convening Council costs nothing and gains cues and promotions.
* Not established: that this holds on other kinds of lives (only two seeds of one curriculum
  generator); that the substrate's own detectors have anything to say about a developmental
  life (they found only claim contradictions); anything about granting broader authority.
* The real organism still has no authority path from questions. That stays William's decision;
  the fork shows what it would do.
