# Generic folding v0: one rule at every depth (opt-in, IMPLEMENTED-EXPERIMENTAL)

First slice of the Generalized Recursive Scaling archive, as `sidecar_fold`. No engine file edited.
REIFY: a promoted structure gets a handle concept (`kernel.ensure_concept`, as object promotion does).
USE: when an experience contains every member of an available fold, the workspace binding is routed
through the handle, repeatedly. FOLD: handles bond with what they are held with; the unchanged
structure observer, gates and Council decide. A handle never bonds with its own contents.
Depth is not in the code; it is measured afterwards.

## OBSERVED (`run_observer_recursive_fold.py`, seed 2301, 107 lessons, whole experimental stack:
recurrence + bond clock + rehearsal + surprise + wave + fold)
| archive gate | result |
|---|---|
| A level independence | 20 structures, all `StructurePromotionEvent`: 16 at depth 1 (3 words), 3 at depth 2 (9 words), 1 at depth 3 (27 words) |
| B repeatability | same run twice: same fingerprint |
| C selective reuse | a new scene of a taught group routes to its one fold; a near-miss scene (two of its families + another) does not recruit it |
| D/F causal, lineage | removing one depth-1 fold: world routes to 7 things instead of 1, scene to 5 instead of 1, recall from that family 3 words instead of 9; restoring it returns 1 / 1 / 9 |
| H work | a 27-concept lesson is held in mind as 3 things while being learned and as 1 once folded; a 9-concept scene as 3, then 1 |
| refusals | a group taught together only twice did not fold; a group taught 5 times in one context did not fold; no unintended fold |
Same stack without `sidecar_fold`: 16 structures, none larger than 3 concepts, no scene, no world.

## Not in this slice (the archive's own open items)
saturation and SPLIT; UNZIP/REVISE of a fold whose evidence changed (the existing refold tools were
not exercised); partial recognition of a fold; future-option value; folding from sight or sound;
anything beyond a prepared, blocked curriculum. One seed.
