# The wave's meaning, first pieces: the unknown, stance and tension (Opus, 2026-11-17)

Origin notes tested: phase is "synergy or conflict among facets"; -M is anti-memory, the unknown;
contradiction is "carried, not erased". V4 had the wave formula with random frequencies and phases;
V5 has a working field memory where phase is only an address and the readout keeps strength only.

## 1. The unknown (-M) = what the knowns cannot explain (read-only, real kernel)
`run_observer_unknown_residual.py`: share of an experience's field effect left after projecting
onto the known concepts' resonance profiles. Text, 30 families, seed 2301 (best-3 projection):
0/3 words new 0.228, 1/3 0.252, 2/3 0.276, 3/3 0.282 (sd ~0.02). Graded, rises at every step, small.
On sight (`run_observer_one_door.py`, see ONE_DOOR_V0.md): separates never-shown from known shapes
in 95-100% of pairs on 3 seeds (92% on raw eye numbers); the stand-out measure gave 23-96%.

## 2. Stance and tension in the field (`sidecar_stance`, opt-in, 3 tests)
Write: a claim's proposition address goes into the field in step (affirmed) or half a turn round
(denied). Record: inside the same experience, a proposition-stance concept keeps what the field
received along that address, lifelong (sum, sum of sizes) and recent (keeps half per telling).
Never reads or writes claim records; tested that the claims engine's records are identical.

`run_observer_stance.py`, seed 2301, 304 lessons, 8 propositions of each kind:
| kind | field reads it right | net | tension | claims engine belief |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| told true x4 | 8/8 | 0.38 | 0.00 | affirmed |
| told false x4 | 8/8 | 0.40 | 0.00 | negated |
| contested | 8/8 | 0.08 | 0.30 | none (contradiction record) |
| true early, false late | 8/8 | 0.06 | 0.31 | none (contradiction record) |
| told once | 8/8 | 0.40 | 0.00 | affirmed |
| never claimed | 8/8 | 0 | 0 | none (no record) |
Field and claims engine agree on which propositions are in conflict: 40/40. Tension ranks every
conflicted proposition above every settled one. Same result with the wave and the whole
experimental heartbeat installed. Recent stance: 8/8 reversed propositions lean "false".

What this does and does not show: the field largely records what it was told (stance is written
and read along the same address), so agreement is expected. It shows the signal survives the real
field write cleanly and carries conflict as a continuous quantity without consulting the engine.
Not done yet: tension feeding attention; contested vs reversed are not separable by lifelong stance.
