Verdant-Minds: A Hybrid Cognitive Architecture with Graph Memory, Wave-State Dynamics, and Measurable Emergent Concept Scaffolding

Abstract
We present Verdant-Minds, a hybrid cognitive architecture that combines graph memory, wave-state dynamics (ECWF), and governance-mediated control to cultivate and analyze emergent conceptual structure. We report a reproducible temporal scaffolding effect in emergent-to-emergent links: in v2 orientation, older emergent concepts act as parents/scaffolds for newer emergent concepts. Across 20/20 seeds, shuffle-null z-scores exceed 2 (mean 2.96 ± 0.86). A deep run yields earlier-share 1.000, shuffle-null z≈7.20, and degree-preserving-null z≈96.

Key results
- 20/20 seeded runs with z > 2.
- Earlier-share mean 1.000 (std 0.000).
- Two-timescale edge-age mixture: ~1190 s and ~98313 s modes; ΔBIC strongly favors two-component model.

Limitations
Seed dependence, graph-density artifacts, pconnect sensitivity, LLM variability, and non-causal interpretation.
