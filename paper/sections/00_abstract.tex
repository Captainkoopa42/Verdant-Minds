\begin{abstract}
We present Verdant-Minds, a hybrid cognitive architecture that combines graph memory, wave-state dynamics (ECWF), and governance-mediated control to cultivate and analyze emergent conceptual structure. We report a reproducible temporal scaffolding effect in emergent-to-emergent links: newly formed emergent concepts show a strong bias toward linking to older emergent concepts. Across 20/20 seeds, shuffle-null z-scores exceed 2 ($\bar{z}=2.96\pm0.86$). A deep run yields earlier-share $=1.000$, shuffle-null $z\approx7.20$, and degree-preserving-null $z\approx96$. We provide methods, null models, and reproducibility assets suitable for Overleaf and Zenodo submission.
\end{abstract}
