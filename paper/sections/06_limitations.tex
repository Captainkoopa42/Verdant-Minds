\section{Limitations}
Key limitations include:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Dependence on seeded concept initialization.
  \item Potential graph-density artifacts in highly connected states.
  \item Sensitivity to \texttt{pconnect} edge policy.
  \item Variability introduced by external LLM behavior during cultivation.
  \item Correlation does not prove causal mechanism.
\end{itemize}

Our current hypothesis is therefore framed as an analogy: Verdant's temporal scaffolding resembles citation-network aging dynamics where new nodes preferentially attach to older nodes, but additional interventions are needed for causal attribution.
